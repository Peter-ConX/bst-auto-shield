## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, dark luxury aesthetic engineered specifically for high-end automotive protection, paint protection films (PPF), ceramic coatings, and architectural-grade window tinting. The visual environment mirrors the sterile, climate-controlled cleanrooms of exotic hypercar studios rather than conventional repair garages.

### Design Movement: Dark Technical Luxury & Precision Glass
The aesthetic blends precision engineering with technical glassmorphism. It uses deep matte surfaces, razor-thin translucent borders, subtle optical refractions, and focused cyan/electric-blue light paths to evoke durability, optical clarity, and aerospace-grade craftsmanship.

### Target Audience & Emotional Tone
- **Audience:** Owners of supercars, luxury performance vehicles, and modern electric vehicles seeking pristine protection without aesthetic compromise.
- **Atmosphere:** Controlled, ultra-clean, clinical, bespoke, uncompromisingly technical.
- **User Reaction:** "My vehicle is being treated in an advanced cleanroom facility by master technicians."

## Colors

The palette is anchored in deep light-absorbent tones layered with subtle metallic neutral tints, punctuated by high-tension electric cyan energy.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Obsidian Ground (`#0B0C10`):** The primary canvas background. Absorbs glare, providing maximum contrast for vehicle photography and gloss finishes.
- **Matte Carbon (`#121418`):** Base layer for foundational cards, app sidebars, and inactive surfaces.
- **Deep Carbon Sheet (`#1C1F26`):** Elevated container surfaces, flyouts, and active panel segments.
- **Brushed Gunmetal (`#242933`):** Surface borders, dividers, card strokes, and subtle hover backgrounds.
- **Dark Gunmetal (`#2D333F`):** Structural outlines, interactive control containers, and disabled UI tokens.

### Accent & Precision Signals
- **Electric Cyan (`#00E5FF`):** The primary focal point. Used strictly for core interactive calls-to-action, active indicators, shield-grade metrics, and laser-precise highlights.
- **Aero Blue (`#0EA5E9`):** Secondary gradient stops, directional shadows, secondary actions, and optical layer badges.

### Text & Illumination
- **Pure Ice (`#F3F4F6`):** Primary headings, key metrics, and selected states. Crisp and anti-reflective.
- **Muted Titanium (`#94A3B8`):** Body text, secondary labels, and descriptive specs.
- **Dark Slate (`#64748B`):** Footnotes, technical watermarks, and subtle auxiliary information.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs architectural geometry with engineered legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers clean technical authority with subtle geometric quirks that mirror modern aerodynamic automotive design. Kept strictly uppercase or sentence-cased depending on hierarchy level.
- **Body & Editorial (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Modern, sharp, and highly legible across dense specification breakdowns, warranty guidelines, and process descriptions.
- **Technical Readouts & Micro-labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to mil thicknesses, UV rejection percentages, infrared heat retention indices, VIN numbers, and status tags to anchor the clinical engineering tone.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an uncompromising 12-column dynamic grid designed for precision alignment and generous architectural breathing room.

- **Desktop Layout:** 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `3rem` (`48px`) margins. Section compositions maintain generous vertical breathing space (`space-2xl`) to highlight pristine surface treatments without visual clutter.
- **Tablet Layout (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile Layout (<768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` margins. Dense technical comparisons convert into horizontally swipeable cards with fixed snap alignment.

## Elevation & Depth

To convey automotive glass coatings and advanced paint protection, depth relies on physical light transmission rather than standard drop shadows.

### Glass Translucency & Specular Boundaries
Cards and floating panels use layered backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) combined with alpha-blended backgrounds (`rgba(28, 31, 38, 0.72)`). Every raised card features an ultra-thin 1px top border simulating a precision chamfered edge:
`border-top: 1px solid rgba(243, 244, 246, 0.15)`
and a subtle foundation edge:
`border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.

### Cyan Light Refraction
Active states, hovering inspection panels, and hero focus elements cast a directed cold glow:
`box-shadow: 0 0 35px -5px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.18), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.4)`.
This evokes laser alignments and sterile UV curing lamps used in cleanrooms.

## Shapes

The design uses tight, controlled, mechanical corner radii (`roundedness: 1`). 

- **Base Radii (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for micro-badges, mono spec chips, form inputs, and checkboxes.
- **Large Radii (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for container cards, configurator tiles, dialog windows, and primary action buttons.
- **Pill/Full Radii (Reserved):** Strictly reserved for vehicle tint percentage selector nodes and live status indicators (e.g., curing status). Avoid rounded, bubbly containers to maintain high-precision, technical aesthetics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Electric Action:** Solid `#00E5FF` background with `#0B0C10` high-contrast typography in bold mono or geometric weights. Micro-hover triggers a clean light bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.45)`) and slight upward lift (1px).
- **Secondary Carbon Action:** `#1C1F26` background, 1px border of `#2D333F`, Pure Ice text. Hover shifts border color to `#00E5FF` at 50% opacity.
- **Ghost Specification Action:** Transparent surface with bottom-aligned subtle rule and inline JetBrains Mono chevron (`->`).

### Package & Specification Cards
- Structured with `#121418` matte interiors, bounded by 1px `#242933` precision borders.
- Header zones integrate a mono badge displaying protection layer attributes (e.g., `10 MIL SELF-HEALING URETHANE`).
- Active or hovered package cards illuminate an edge gradient shifting from `#00E5FF` to transparent along the left or top border.

### Interactive Tint & Film Sliders
- Custom track in deep gunmetal (`#242933`) with active fill rendered in `#00E5FF`.
- Thumb controller is an engineered brushed metal ring with a glass core, displaying the exact VLT (Visible Light Transmission) percentage dynamically (e.g., `70%`, `35%`, `20%`, `5%`) in `label-mono-sm`.

### Form Fields & Technical Inputs
- Background: `#121418` with an inset shadow simulating a recessed chassis plate.
- Idle Border: 1px `#242933`.
- Focused Border: 1px `#00E5FF` with a subtle 3px cyan focus halo.
- Labels: Small uppercase mono styling (`#94A3B8`) sitting directly above the input container.

### Status Chips & Inspection Badges
- Compact height (24px) utilizing mono typography.
- Filled with semi-translucent gunmetal (`rgba(45, 51, 63, 0.5)`) and bounded by micro-borders matched to protection status:
  - Hydrophobic Certified: Cyan accent
  - UV 99% Rejection: Cyan accent
  - In-Curing / Scheduled: Slate accent
